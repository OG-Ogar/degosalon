How booking works

  1. Register as a customer and sign in.
  2. Open Find a barber to see every barber taking bookings.
  3. Pick a style, choose a date and time, and send the request.
  4. The barber accepts or declines it, and you see the result under My bookings.
  5. Message the barber at any point from Messages.
