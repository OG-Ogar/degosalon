Find and book a barber

Choose your state/FCT and optional LGA or area council, open a barber’s profile, and choose a service. Sign in as a customer to select a date and an available time, then submit your appointment request. Check My Bookings for its status.

Messages

Open Messages from your dashboard. Send a message or use Refresh conversation to see new replies.

Products and collection

Open Products from your customer dashboard, choose available items and review your cart before placing an order. Follow the collection and payment instructions shown with your order.

Account recovery
